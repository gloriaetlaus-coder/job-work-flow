🇨🇳 中文话术
1 "请简单做个自我介绍" 3句话说清你是谁、做过什么、想干什么
您好，我是黄文艳。过去8年我主要负责对接客户和统筹项目。服务过戴森、依云、洲际酒店等品牌。我的核心工作是跨部门沟通和跟进落地，擅长把复杂的客户需求，从方案制定一直推进到最终的交付和复盘。

今年gap期间因为个人兴趣，自学研究了大部分主流AI工具，用过国外的claude、chatgpt、各种ai buider和国内agent等，在零代码背景下，vibe coding跑通过网站、小工具和工作台，这也让我对当前AI的实操逻辑有比较深的理解，这也是我0-1能力的充分证明。

我之所以对这个岗位感兴趣，是因为目前在尝试职业能力上的升级，与其在原来的行业里做重复的事，我更希望能带着过去积累的能力，投身到新的行业趋势里来。在过去的工作过程中，我形成了一套成熟的方法论和做事体系，可以用最短的时间熟悉产品，并且用落地的思维去推进业务。我希望在这个行业里长期发展，跟公司一起成长。
2 "你的经验跟我们公司的xx岗位不一样？" 承认差异，但核心能力互通
工作内容确实有差异。但我过去8年沉淀的核心能力，其实跟贵司是互通的。我以前对接的客户，像依云、洲际，很多甲方的流程都非常繁琐，内部部门也很多。
我作为项目的总接口人，负责拉通各方、控制进度、排除风险。这其实和贵司在给大客户做项目交付、做客户成功时的底层逻辑是一样的。
我唯一需要去适应和补足的，是咱们具体产品里的专业术语和行业知识。通常来说，给我两周时间，我就可以把产品逻辑吃透，并把它翻译成客户听得懂的价值。
3 "你为什么从上一份工作离职？" 用"个人发展"导向，引出转型AI叙事
跟前公司的合同自然到期了。其实很感谢前老板，很爽快的给了我补偿金，让我有了一定的经济底气，能沉下心来去学习和思考真正想做什么，所以我也很珍惜这段时间，最终确定了自己要转型的想法。
因为我基本一毕业就一直在这类行业工作，可以说所有的工作流程非常熟悉了。今年对这个xx方向很感兴趣，所以想往这个赛道转型，想找一个能让我把以往经验和新业务结合的岗位。
4 "你对这个岗位/我们公司了解多少？" 查公司主营业务，嵌进岗位关键词
我了解过咱们公司主要做xx，这个岗位需要xx。我之前在B端做了8年客户对接，从需求收集到交付跟进全链路都做过，今年也在用AI工具做项目落地，觉得个人经验和贵司的需求挺匹配的。
5 "你的期望薪资是多少？" 给区间不给死数，留谈判空间
因为目前在做转型，所以我的基础期望薪资会在13-16K之间，具体可以根据岗位的实际职责和团队情况来谈，我更看重的是这个岗位的发展空间和机会。
6 "你有什么问题想问我们的吗？" 问业务相关问题，展现思考深度
想问一下这个岗位日常主要对接哪些部门？
团队目前在做哪些尝试？
我如果加入的话，前三个月的主要目标是什么？
团队目前的工作节奏大概是怎样的？
公司盈利情况/试用期考核+五险一金标准+团队配置+晋升机制？
公司目前处于的这个阶段，对xx部门的核心考核指标和未来一年的业务增长预期是什么？
这个岗位所支持的品牌或产品线，在公司内部属于哪一类业务？（核心成熟业务/新兴增长业务）
公司对这块业务的资源投入和长期规划是怎样的？
可以描述一下一个典型的项目从提报、审批到执行的跨部门协作流程吗？（这能听出流程是否高效）。
🌍 English Scripts
1 "Tell me about yourself." 3 sentences: who you are, what you've done, what you want
Hello, I'm Huang Wenyan. Over the past 8 years, I've been mainly responsible for client management and project coordination. I've served brands like Dyson, Evian, and InterContinental Hotels. My core work is cross-department communication and driving execution — I'm good at taking complex client requirements from solution design all the way through to final delivery and retrospective.

During my gap period this year, driven by personal interest, I self-studied most of the mainstream AI tools — including Claude, ChatGPT, various AI builders, and domestic AI agents. With a zero-code background, I used vibe coding to build websites, small tools, and workbenches. This gave me a fairly deep understanding of the practical logic behind current AI, and it's also strong proof of my 0-to-1 capability.

The reason I'm interested in this role is that I'm currently looking to upgrade my professional skills. Rather than continuing to do repetitive work in my original industry, I'd rather bring my accumulated capabilities into new industry trends. Over the course of my past work, I've developed a mature methodology and way of working — I can get familiar with a product in the shortest possible time and drive business with a practical, execution-oriented mindset. I hope to grow long-term in this industry and grow together with the company.
2 "Your experience doesn't match our company's XX role?" Acknowledge difference, pivot to transferable skills
The work content does differ. But the core capabilities I've built over the past 8 years are actually transferable to your company. The clients I worked with — like Evian and InterContinental — many of them had very complex processes and many internal departments.
As the project's main point of contact, I was responsible for aligning all parties, controlling timelines, and eliminating risks. This shares the same underlying logic as your company's project delivery and customer success work with large clients.
The only thing I need to adapt to and fill in is the specific terminology and industry knowledge around your product. Typically, give me two weeks, and I can fully grasp the product logic and translate it into value that customers understand.
3 "Why did you leave your last job?" Frame it as growth-oriented
My contract with my previous company naturally expired. I'm actually very grateful to my former boss — he straightforwardly gave me a severance package, which gave me the financial cushion to settle down and learn and think about what I really want to do. So I really value this period, and ultimately made the decision to transition.
I've basically been working in this type of industry since graduation, so I can say I'm very familiar with all the workflows. This year I became very interested in the XX direction, so I want to transition into this track and find a role where I can combine my past experience with new business.
4 "What do you know about this role / our company?" Research company, embed JD keywords
I've looked into your company — you mainly do XX, and this role requires XX. I've done 8 years of B2B client work, covering the full chain from requirement gathering to delivery follow-up. This year I've also been using AI tools for project implementation, and I think my personal experience aligns well with your needs.
5 "What are your salary expectations?" Give a range, not a fixed number
Since I'm currently transitioning, my base salary expectation is in the 13-16K range. It's negotiable based on the actual scope of the role and the team situation. What I value more is the development space and opportunities this role offers.
6 "Do you have any questions for us?" Ask business-related questions to show depth
Which departments does this role primarily work with on a daily basis?
What initiatives is the team currently working on?
If I join, what would be the main goals for the first three months?
What's the current work pace of the team like?
Company profitability / probation assessment + social insurance standards + team configuration + promotion mechanism?
At the company's current stage, what are the core KPIs and one-year business growth expectations for the XX department?
Which category does the brand or product line this role supports fall under within the company? (Core mature business / emerging growth business)
What's the company's resource investment and long-term planning for this business area?
Could you describe a typical cross-department project workflow from proposal, approval, to execution? (This can reveal whether the process is efficient.)
